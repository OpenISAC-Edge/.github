We are building a shared research platform that connects sensing, wireless communication, and edge AI. Our goal is to help researchers run reproducible ISAC experiments across devices and testbeds, with hardware constraints built into the workflow.

## Meet the team

## Collaborate with us

We welcome researchers, developers, and hardware teams. You can help with hardware adapters and edge deployment, ISAC algorithms and AI control, O-RAN integration, or experiment automation and documentation. We are also interested in collaborators who can contribute testbed experience and reproducible experiment recipes.

Tell us about your team, the problem you are working on, and how you would like to get involved. Use **Get a Demo** or **Contribute** on our website, or email [xxue@hawaii.edu](mailto:xxue@hawaii.edu) directly.
